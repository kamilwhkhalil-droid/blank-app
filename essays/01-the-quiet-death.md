# The Quiet Death

*Board Reading No. 1 in a six-part series on how organizations die.*

---

In 2007, Nokia held roughly half of the world's smartphone market. Its own engineers had built touchscreen prototypes that outperformed the first iPhone. The prototypes existed. The data existed. The knowledge never reached the executive floor.

By 2013, Nokia sold its phone division for scrap.

Here is the reading Nokia's board never saw, because nobody was scoring it:

**High Potential. Negative Velocity.**

Potential is your current capacity to create value — market position, operational strength, adaptive capacity. Nokia's in 2007 was a 9 out of 10. Velocity is the *rate of change* of that Potential — and Nokia's was already negative.

A 9 and a minus sign. That is the most dangerous reading an organization can produce, and I call it the Quiet Death, because every number except one says you are winning — and the one that disagrees is small, abstract, and easy to explain away for exactly as long as it would have mattered.

This is the reading's cruelty: it never forces a response. A -0.5 Velocity is always explainable. Seasonality. One bad hire. A soft quarter. Each explanation is individually reasonable, and the sum of the explanations is an obituary. Nokia's Potential collapsed from 9 to 4 in four years, and at no single point in those four years did any quarter look bad enough to justify panic.

Revenue will not warn you. Revenue is a lagging indicator — Blockbuster's revenue was still climbing toward its $5.9 billion peak while its Potential was already collapsing. Potential measures the derivative of relevance, not the absolute of revenue. By the time the absolute turns, the derivative has been negative for years.

The defense is not intelligence. Nokia had plenty. The defense is a structure that forces the small negative number onto the table before it is large enough to be undeniable — because "undeniable" and "too late" arrive in the same quarter.

Score your Velocity this week. Not your revenue — your Velocity. If the sign is negative and your explanation for it is reasonable, be more worried, not less. Reasonable explanations are what the Quiet Death is made of.
