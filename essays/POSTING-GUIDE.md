# The Six Board Readings — Posting Guide

Six standalone essays, each diagnosing a recognizable corporate death with one board reading
from *The Metabolic Imperative*. Ready to paste into LinkedIn or Substack.

## Order and cadence

Post one per week, in numbered order. The sequence is deliberate:

| # | Essay | Job in the funnel |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | The Quiet Death (Nokia) | Strongest hook — the famous corpse everyone thinks they understand |
| 2 | The Gilded Trap (GM) | The counterintuitive one — "cash-rich and trapped" earns shares |
| 3 | The Converging Lines (Lehman) | The mechanical one — establishes analytical credibility |
| 4 | The Hollow Board (WeWork / Boeing / Credit Suisse) | The contrarian one — "believe your Glassdoor" travels well |
| 5 | Velocity Without a Vector (Quibi) | The flattering-trap one — speaks to every startup reader |
| 6 | The Founder's Board (the author's own company) | The conversion piece — personal, actionable, ends the series on the reader's own board |

Essay 6 is the one that sells: it is the only one where the reader is the case study.

## Before posting

1. **Publish the site first** (GitHub Pages), and confirm the URL. Every essay ends with
   `https://kamilwhkhalil-droid.github.io/blank-app/` — if your Pages URL differs (custom
   domain, renamed repo), find-and-replace it across all six files.
2. **LinkedIn:** paste the body without the `#` heading (LinkedIn has no markdown headers;
   the first line becomes the hook). Keep the line breaks — white space is what makes long
   LinkedIn posts readable. Put the link in the post body, not the first comment, unless
   reach testing suggests otherwise.
3. **Substack:** paste as-is; markdown carries over. Title the series consistently, e.g.
   "How Organizations Die, No. 1: The Quiet Death."
4. **Cross-link:** from essay 2 onward, add one line at the top linking the previous essay.

## Voice notes

- The essays are written in the book's first-person voice. Essays 1–5 use "I" sparingly;
  essay 6 is fully personal. Post them under the author's own profile.
- No author name appears in the files (matching the site). Your platform byline carries it.
